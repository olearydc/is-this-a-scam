## 2. Ranked ideas (15)

Ranking = older-user value against effort. All "browser" ideas can ship as static content in our single page.

1. **Short fixed "do this now" line after every verdict, in the ai'd pattern: label, one reason, one instruction.** Example form: "Likely a scam. It uses urgency and asks you to click a link. Do not click. Ring your bank on the number on your card." Who: ai'd (opened https://getaid.ai/). Why: older users act on one short sentence, not a list. Browser. Low. (We have "what they want" already; the gain is to force reason + single instruction in one box.)

2. **A named "not sure" outcome that routes to a person.** ScamShield uses an explicit "unsure" class (updates page, opened https://reports.open.gov.sg/scamshield/updates), ai'd says "if something still feels uncertain, send to trusted contact", Which? says "a good chance it's a scam". Wording suggestion: "We can't tell. Treat it as a scam until a person you trust has looked at it." plus the helper-send button and the Age Action/Garda numbers. Why: removes the pressure to guess when rules fire weakly. Browser. Low.

3. **"Did you already click / reply / pay?" triage branch after the verdict.** NCSC email page (opened https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/phishing-scams/report-scam-email) separates "responded or clicked" from "lost money" and explains that reporting to NCSC is not a crime report. AARP volunteers give calm "you are not to blame" plus direct "do it today" steps (opened https://www.aarp.org/money/scams-fraud/fraud-watch-network-helpline-volunteers/). Offer three buttons: I only read it / I clicked or replied / I paid or gave codes, each with an Irish step list (bank, Garda, change passwords). Why: the most frightened user is the one who already acted. Browser. Low-Med.

4. **Shame-free, calm tone line and "you are not to blame".** AARP volunteers script it; Fraud Watch page offers confidential support groups; BPFI advice quotes "Don't feel embarrassed" (opened https://bpfi.ie/surge-ai-generated-adverts-promoting-bogus-investment-schemes-fraudsmart/). Malwarebytes "judgment-free" claim is UNVERIFIED. Add a short reassuring line on victim and "already acted" pages. Browser. Low.

5. **"Banks can tell you if the call is real" tip.** UK banks have in-app call checkers (Nationwide, Revolut, Monzo, Starling) and the 159 line (Which? article, opened). For Ireland we have not verified an equivalent number; do NOT add a number without checking. Add generic advice: "Hang up, then use your bank's app or the number on your card, and ask the bank to confirm whether they rang you." Browser. Low. UNVERIFIED for Irish banks' specific in-app call-check features.

6. **Trust-signal block: "We will never ask you for..." plus who made this.** Take Five states "We will never text or call you and ask you to share your information or transfer money" and warns that criminals may impersonate Take Five (opened https://www.takefive-stopfraud.org.uk/). ScamShield says officials never ask for transfers by phone. Add a visible statement: "This page never asks for your name, bank or code. Nothing you paste leaves your phone." plus a named owner and contact. Why: older users judge a checker site itself as possibly a scam. Browser. Low.

7. **Printed/Easy Read "pocket card" version of the five rules.** Scamwatch's book now has Easy Read (search snippet, UNVERIFIED on page); Age UK has a printed information guide but no stated large print (opened https://www.ageuk.org.uk/information-advice/money-legal/scams-fraud/); Take Five has Accessibility toolkit. We already have a leaflet: add a wallet-size "Stop, ring them back, tell someone" card with the Irish numbers. Browser. Low.

8. **"Where did this come from?" first-step picker with a different default advice per channel (text, email, WhatsApp, call, letter, doorstep, pop-up).** Age UK splits by channel (phone, post, doorstep, investment, TV Licence); Scamio accepts offline "describe it" cases. Many Irish scams arrive by letter or doorstep, which our paste box cannot see. Add letter/doorstep/post pages. Browser. Med.

9. **Post-report reassurance: "no reply is normal."** NCSC says it "can't tell you the outcome... but acts on every report" and says no need to forward spam-folder items. Use the same expectations on our report page so people do not feel ignored. ScamShield found push-notification feedback after a report increased reporting (updates page, opened). Browser for the wording. Low. Notification feedback is [server].

10. **Number or website lookup hand-off with honest limits.** Scamchecker.ie (opened https://scamchecker.ie; run by BPFI, developed by ScamAdviser, backed by Irish banks) already linked. Add also "Who Called Me" for phone numbers (Which? list, opened) and a line that the tools "may not easily spot" fakes (Which? warning, opened) and that a clean result is not proof. Browser (links only). Low.

11. **"Why was this flagged?" detail in plain text and optional second line for each warning sign.** ScamShield Android shows why a message was filtered (Q4 2024, opened). F-Secure also returns "justification". We have lit-up signs; make each expandable to a one-sentence plain reason and an example of what a genuine message does instead. Browser. Low-Med.

12. **Opt-in email scam alerts for Irish scams.** Which? weekly Scam Alert emails (search snippet), FraudSMART email alerts (opened). We could add a link to FraudSMART alert sign-up (Low, browser) or run our own list (Med-High, server, GDPR). ScamShield's own opt-in alert trial saw only 13.3% opt-in and was removed 7 Sep 2026 (opened), so do not build our own first. Link only.

13. **Trusted-helper "second opinion" with notification back (two-way).** ai'd Family Plan adds notifications (opened; paid, details thin). Currently our one-tap send is one way. A reply could just be a text from the helper; a structured "Looks OK / Don't touch" reply needs a server or push. Browser version: a pre-written reply template for the helper ("Don't click it. I'll ring you."). Browser Low; real two-way [server] High.

14. **Forward-to-check channel (WhatsApp/SMS/email bot) with rule scan on a server.** Scamio on WhatsApp, ScamShield bot (11,960 sessions in a quarter, so modest uptake), Cofacts LINE bot (search snippet, UNVERIFIED current), NCSC email forwarding. Would break our "nothing leaves your phone" promise unless checks stay stateless and logs are off. Server. High. Recommend only if demand is proven; WhatsApp Business API cost and verification apply (UNVERIFIED).

15. **Community report of Irish scam texts (anonymous counts, no message text).** Truecaller (r1), Who Called Me (user submitted), AARP scam map, Cofacts feedback loop. Gives "others reported this" signal. Server. High, plus moderation and GDPR risk. Defer.

16. **Volunteer or "digital buddy" model for the helpline gap.** AARP helpline relies on 160+ trained volunteers, triage team, 20+ hours of training. Partner with Age Action / Alone / Community Gardaí for a "call us" route and list those existing lines on the phone page rather than starting one. Browser (content). Low. Starting an own helpline is not recommended.

17. **Accessibility toolkit copy from Take Five (UK).** Opened page lists an Accessibility toolkit; contents not read, UNVERIFIED. Worth reading and borrowing formats (large print, audio, easy read). Browser. Low-Med.

18. **Website age / domain reputation check client side.** Not found on any page I opened as a feature beyond ScamAdviser's trust score (Get Safe Online page 403; r0 says 1-100 from 40+ sources). A true website-age lookup needs a WHOIS/RDAP call which exposes the domain to a third party. Server or third-party call. Med. Could be an optional "Check this link" button that opens Scamchecker.ie in a new tab with the link copied (Browser, Low).

19. **Screen-share and remote-access warning in the pop-up/phone pages.** Microsoft Quick Assist makes users tick a risk box (opened); WhatsApp warns before screen-sharing with unknown contacts (Meta article, opened). Add explicit lines: "Never let anyone share your screen or install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or Quick Assist." Browser. Low.

20. **A "bank safe account" and "customs fee" plain myth-busters on a "Things no real company does" page.** FraudSMART story themes: bank "safe" account transfers, customs fees, romance (opened). A short list "A real bank / Garda / Revenue / An Post will never..." (check each against the official source before publishing; none verified here). Browser. Low.

## 3. Caveats
- No page opened gives accuracy rates for any tool except vendor claims (Malwarebytes 15%, McAfee earlier >99%). Do not quote them.
- Norton Genie (ie page), Scamwatch, Get Safe Online, Cofacts, McAfee, Little Black Book all failed to open; anything about them here is UNVERIFIED or taken from r1/r0.
- Irish-specific bank call-check, forwarding short code, and reporting numbers were not confirmed in this round.
- ScamShield data is Singapore-specific; app-only features need native apps we do not have.
- AARP "Fraud Watch" volunteer numbers differ between pages (older: 100+ volunteers/400 calls per day from search snippet; opened page: 160+ volunteers/150 calls per day). Treat as approximate.
