# R1: AI scam-checker apps and tools (research notes)

Date of research: 2026-10-21. Sources are vendor pages, help centres, press and community posts. Where a fetch returned HTTP 403, the claim comes from search snippets only and is marked. Exact verdict labels were found for almost no tool. Treat "not found" as "not in the sources I could read", not as "does not exist".

## Bitdefender Scamio
- Start: free, web app, WhatsApp, Messenger or Discord. Sign-in or a free Bitdefender account is required ("you only need to do this once"). No download. [https://www.bitdefender.com/consumer/scamio] (fetched)
- Input: paste text, links or QR codes; upload screenshots or photos of emails, texts or chats; or describe the scam in words (for example an offline call or letter). [same, fetched]
- Result: no fixed verdict labels found. The page says it "lets you know if it's safe or not" and "signals" suspicious elements. Search snippet says results are generic tips and the AI does not explain why it rated something a scam. [https://www.bitdefender.com/en-au/consumer/scamio via search, unverified in full]
- Follow-up: chat-based, so the user can ask more questions. Ends with a rating of the experience. [search snippet, unverified]
- Next steps: generic avoid-scam tips. No reporting or blocking flow found.
- Family/caregiver: none described. Only "help you and your dear ones easily spot fake messages". [fetched]
- Accessibility: not found.
- Privacy: "We only collect and use your personal data in accordance with our Privacy Policy." No retention period stated. [fetched]
- Cost: free. [fetched]
- Languages: not listed on page (metadata en-US). [fetched]

## Norton Genie (Gen Digital)
- Start: Genie app for iOS and Android, Norton.com web, or ChatGPT (launched March 2026, type "@Norton" then paste). [https://us.norton.com/products/genie-scam-detector (403, via search); https://community.norton.com/t/introducing-norton-genie/235685 (fetched); ChatGPT via https://futurefive.co.nz/story/norton-launches-genie-scam-check-tool-in-chatgpt-nz (search)]
- Input: copy and paste text, or upload a screenshot. Also works for email, social posts and websites. [community post, fetched]
- Result: tells you if the message is "potentially a scam and why", usually within seconds. Also says what the criminals seem to be aiming for and suggests next steps. If unsure, gives guidance on how to judge it yourself. [community post, fetched]
- ChatGPT version categories (search snippet, unverified in full): "safe", "risky", "likely a scam", with next steps such as not replying, avoiding links, deleting the message.
- Follow-up: the ChatGPT version is a chat. Standalone app Q&A is described in product copy ("answer any scam questions"). [search snippet]
- Detection: language and context plus URL and domain checks; claims to look beyond blocklists. [search snippet of Norton release, unverified]
- Caveat: "may not always determine whether something is a scam". [fetched]
- Family/caregiver, accessibility: not found.
- Privacy: page says "Every time you use it, you're helping Genie get smarter." It does not say what is uploaded, stored or kept. [fetched]
- Cost: "for free at Norton.com"; app cost not stated. Early-access wording in a 2025-era post. [fetched, unverified currency]
- Languages: not stated.
- Availability: disclaimer lists US, UK, Ireland, Australia, NZ. Requires iOS 15+ or Android 8+. [fetched]

## Malwarebytes Scam Guard
- Start: Malwarebytes app. Desktop: sidebar Scam Guard icon, start a chat. Mobile: app, Security, "AI Scam Guard". [https://help.malwarebytes.com/hc/en-us/articles/36738112474395 (403 on fetch, search snippet only)]
- Input: screenshot. Paperclip button, pick the image (mobile may ask for photo permission), tap Add, then the blue arrow to send. [search snippet, unverified in full]
- Result: bot says whether the item is "suspicious or a known threat". Exact labels not found.
- Follow-up: may offer to send the item to Malwarebytes' threat intelligence team for analysis. [search snippet]
- Caveat: AI verdicts "aren't always right"; community notes miss newer or targeted scams. [search snippet]
- Family/caregiver, accessibility, privacy, languages, cost: not found in sources read. Malwarebytes help-centre article on account detection exists (https://help.malwarebytes.com/hc/en-us/articles/50590805006875) but returned 403.

## Trend Micro ScamCheck
- Older (2021): chat checker via Messenger, WhatsApp or Chrome extension. Free, 24/7. Links checked for safety; text checked against fact-checking partners. [https://news.trendmicro.com/2021/02/24/how-does-scamcheck-work/ via search]
- Newer app (2025): Scam Radar uses AI to spot fear, urgency and fake-authority tactics. Runs in background on calls, texts and websites. Needs Call Block, SMS Filter and Web Guard switched on. Warning is "clear, customized" with recommended actions. Exact wording not found. [https://discussions.trendmicro.com/en-us/announcement/scamcheck/2025/06/ via search]
- Whether the 2021 chat tool still runs alongside the app: not stated in sources.
- Vendor claim: proprietary AI model. Unverified.
- Family, accessibility, privacy, cost, languages: not found.

## F-Secure Text Message Checker
- Free online checker, separate from paid product. Paid F-Secure Total does automatic blocking. [https://www.f-secure.com/en/text-message-checker via search, fetched not attempted]
- Detection: AI-based. Non-English text translated by AWS Translate under a "no-trace" policy. Verdicts kept in limited form; personal data linked to a message anonymised within a month. Only non-personal data (verdict and justification) used to train AI. [search snippet of vendor page, unverified in full]
- Feedback: thumbs up or down on each result. [search snippet]
- Result wording, layout, next steps: not found.
- Family, accessibility, cost (free online), languages: partly found; not verified in full.

## McAfee Scam Detector
- Upload screenshot, message or link for "on-demand scam check". Analyses full context, not just links. [https://www.mcafee.com/blogs/mcafee-news/how-scam-detector-stops-qr-code-scams-quishing/ via search]
- Android: messages scanned on arrival, flagged before opening. iPhone: suspicious texts moved to separate folder; manual check available. [https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2025/05/14/mcafees-scam-detector/ via search]
- Accuracy: vendor claims over 99% for text detection and about 1.5% of texts flagged. Unverified.
- Availability: included in all core plans at no extra cost (launch press). Pricing/region for Ireland: not checked.
- Result wording, follow-up, family, accessibility, privacy: not found.

## Aura
- Scans incoming texts and moves suspicious ones to junk. Checks links for malware or phishing. [https://www.aura.com/learn/smishing via search, marketing page]
- Limits: sophisticated scams may still get through (Aura's own page). Spam Message Protection iOS only per one help page; another says blocking is US iPhone only. Conflicting, check before use.
- Cost: plans from $9.99/month; call and text protection is an add-on. [search snippet, may be out of date]
- Manual check tool, verdict wording, family: not found.

## Avast / AVG Scam Guardian
- Free. Desktop: built into Avast Free Antivirus (update to latest). Mobile: free Scam Guardian with Avast Mobile Security (Android and iOS). Scam Guardian Pro (call, email, SMS screening) in Premium. [https://newsroom.gendigital.com/2025-06-25-Avast-Makes-AI-Driven-Scam-Defense-Available-for-Free-Worldwide via search]
- Manual check: Avast Assistant chat. Ask questions, upload screenshots or links, get "clear, simple advice on your next move", 24/7. [https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/avast-launches-another-free-scam-protection-tool-but-this-one-is-powered-by-ai-heres-how-to-use-it via search]
- Web Guard checks sites against known dangerous URLs. Email Guard is Pro.
- Verdict wording, family, accessibility, privacy: not found. Sources are mostly vendor press; check Avast support pages.

## Google: Circle to Search scam check (Android) and Google Lens
- Steps: activate Circle to Search (long-press home or nav bar), circle the suspicious text. Alternative: screenshot, then Google Lens. [https://9to5google.com/2025/12/02/circle-to-search-detect-scams-with-ai-overview/ (fetched); Google post October 2026 via https://chromeunboxed.com/googles-circle-to-search-can-now-instantly-tell-you-if-a-text-message-is-a-scam/ (search)]
- Result: opens an AI Overview explaining possible red flags, with "relevant guidance and suggested next steps". Wording quoted: "letting you know if the content is likely a scam". [fetched]
- Next steps (Google post, search snippet): consider deleting the message and blocking the number if red flags show.
- Links: checks whether links point to official sites. [search snippet]
- Warnings only at "high confidence". [fetched]
- Availability varies by device and region. Rolling out globally, possibly slowly. [fetched]
- Gemini on Pixel (Scam Detection, Gemini Nano) covers calls on-device; Google says same approach is coming to Google Messages. Call detection is separate from Circle to Search. [search snippet]
- Family, accessibility, languages, privacy of Circle to Search: not found.

## Apple iPhone: Filter Unknown Senders (built-in)
- Turn on: Settings > Messages > Message Filtering > Filter Unknown Senders. [search snippet, unverified in full]
- Result: messages from non-contacts go to Filters > Unknown Senders. Links in messages from unknown senders cannot be opened until the sender is added or you reply. [search snippet]
- Limits: can hide genuine messages (banks, parcel firms, verification codes). Check that list. [search snippet]
- Report: Report Junk in iMessage; blocking the number stops messages. [search snippet]
- iOS 26: optional Filter Spam uses on-device detection. [search snippet]
- Siri or fake Apple Support scams: not covered in results.
- No caregiver or AI verdict feature. No verdict wording.

## Truecaller Scam Checker (web and Android, new)
- Launched 28 September 2026. Free. Paste a phone number, link or message. [https://techcrunch.com/?p=3169953 and https://justbeingresourceful.com/2026/10/06/truecaller-scam-checker-how-to-check-any-suspicious-link-number-or-message-for-free/ via search]
- Links: expands short URLs and follows redirects, checks against risk database and community reports. [search snippet]
- Message: picks out number or link, shows community reports. Screenshots not yet supported (planned). [search snippet]
- Availability: sources conflict. Android app starts in India; web works globally and needs no account (one source). Check before promising Ireland.
- No false positive or negative rates published.
- Truecaller Assistant is call screening, not a Gemini link. Verified from search snippet only.

## Whoscall (Gogolook, Taiwan)
- Check feature: numbers, links, screenshots. Upload screenshots of WhatsApp, Facebook or WeChat chats or ads. AI gives "risk alerts and advice". [search snippet of app listing/press, unverified in full]
- Caller ID and blocking using global database and crowd reports. Badge system rewards community reporters.
- Privacy: no policy details found in results. Historic 2014 privacy controversy about public records. Check current policy before recommending.
- Company says it identified over 6 billion unknown calls and SMS in 2025 (company figure).
- Verdict wording, follow-up, family, accessibility, cost, languages: not found.

## Ideas for us

1. **Verdict first, in plain words, with a "why" line.** Copy Norton Genie's "potentially a scam and why" pattern and the Circle to Search "likely a scam" wording. Never say "safe". Tools: Norton Genie, Google Circle to Search. Helps: older people get one clear sentence first, then the reason. Needs server: no.
2. **Honest "I can't tell" state.** Norton Genie gives guidance to judge it yourself when unsure. Tools: Norton Genie. Helps: builds trust with people who are wary of tech. Needs server: no.
3. **Route by where the message came from.** Scamio asks for pasted text, a screenshot or a description. Circle to Search and Lens use the screenshot path. Our page could show one step: "Paste the words or upload a picture." Tools: Scamio, Circle to Search. Needs server: no.
4. **Circle-the-text pattern as a one-tap help.** Circle to Search lets users circle text in another app. Our equivalent is a "how to copy this message" guide with pictures for the phone they own. Tools: Circle to Search, Malwarebytes desktop. Needs server: no.
5. **Next-steps card with a fixed short list.** Norton: stop replying, avoid links, delete. Circle to Search: consider deleting and blocking the number. Our card should match Irish reporting steps. Tools: Norton Genie, Circle to Search. Needs server: no.
6. **Feedback thumbs on each result.** F-Secure asks users to like or dislike results, and uses that to train AI. For us: a thumbs up or down that is stored only in the browser or sent nowhere. Tools: F-Secure. Needs server: no for the UI; yes if we want to learn from it.
7. **Explicit privacy promise next to the input.** Scamio and Norton are vague about retention. F-Secure is specific (anonymised within a month). For us, the simpler claim "nothing is uploaded, checked in your browser" is stronger. Tools: F-Secure (contrast). Needs server: no.
8. **Caveat line on every result.** Malwarebytes and Norton both say the AI can be wrong. Put a short line under each verdict: "This is a check, not a guarantee. If in doubt, phone the bank on the number on your card." Tools: Malwarebytes, Norton. Needs server: no.
9. **Filter Unknown Senders warning.** Apple's setting can hide real bank or parcel texts. Add a line: "Check the Unknown Senders list for real messages." Tools: iPhone Messages. Needs server: no.
10. **Caregiver option.** No tool found a real caregiver mode in these sources, except Scamio mentioning "dear ones". Idea: a "Show this to a family member" printable or link-free summary. Tools: none (gap). Needs server: no.
11. **Stay out of the market for paid add-ons.** All top rivals are free only for the checker, with paid blocking (F-Secure Total, Aura, Avast Pro). Our free checker is a clear differentiator. Tools: F-Secure, Aura, Avast. Needs server: no.
12. **Irish region check.** Norton lists Ireland in its availability note. Truecaller's checker is not yet in Ireland per sources. Our page is an Irish-first advantage. Tools: Norton, Truecaller. Needs server: no.
