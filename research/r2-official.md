- VERIFIED tagline: "Informed. Alert. Secure."
- VERIFIED: "Stay in control, don't be rushed and make a decision you or your business will regret." (Be Informed)

**An Garda Siochana fraud advice** - https://www.garda.ie/en/crime/fraud/
- VERIFIED: "Never give personal, financial or security information to persons who are unknown to you."
- VERIFIED: "If it sounds too good to be true it probably is."
- VERIFIED: "Do your research and accept nothing as fact until you have satisfied yourself that it is genuine."
- VERIFIED: "Never click on a link or attachment in an unsolicited email."
- VERIFIED: "Don't respond to any unsolicited email seeking personal, financial or security advice."
- VERIFIED: Gardai and banks will never ask for your banking PIN or password, ask you to transfer money, or come to your home to collect your card, cheque book or cash.
- VERIFIED: Verify any request using contact details you find yourself, not the ones the caller or sender gave.
- VERIFIED reporting: local Garda station (first point of contact; also if unsure whether you have been defrauded). Garda Confidential 1800 666 111. Garda HQ +353 1 666 0000. Emergency 999 or 112.
- VERIFIED "If you have been scammed" steps (the page's own list):
  1. Contact your bank immediately to limit losses; ask them to close or freeze any affected card or account.
  2. Check statements for unfamiliar transactions, especially foreign or online ones.
  3. Report to your local Garda station and keep evidence (emails, messages).
  4. Report compromised social media or communication accounts to the company.
  5. If unsure whether you have been defrauded, report anyway.
- Note: the page also lists an Anti-Bribery and Corruption Unit line (1800 40 60 80). Not a scam line; do not use it for scam reporting.

**ComReg - scam text messages and SMS Sender ID Registry** - https://www.comreg.ie/industry/electronic-communications/nuisance-communications/sms-sender-id-registry
- VERIFIED: the registry is a secure repository of registered SMS Sender IDs; organisations sending texts in Ireland must register their Sender IDs.
- VERIFIED: 3 July 2025: texts from unregistered Sender IDs began to be labelled "Likely Scam".
- VERIFIED: blocking was scheduled for 3 October 2025, but ComReg deferred it (update dated 19 September 2025). No new blocking date given on the page. Do NOT state that texts are blocked.
- VERIFIED: registry figures at 15 October 2026: 22,092 Sender IDs and 16,301 Sender ID owners.
- VERIFIED consumer line: (01) 804 9668, consumerline@comreg.ie (from comreg.ie contact page, https://www.comreg.ie/faq/what-is-comregs-advice-to-consumers-about-scam-sms-text-messages - the FAQ fetch itself did NOT mention scam texts; treat the number as from the contact page, re-check).
- NOT VERIFIED: a forwarding number for scam texts in Ireland. The ComReg pages checked do not list one. Searches did not confirm 50050 for any operator. The search summary says the 50050 claim could not be confirmed.
- Operator data (not an official reporting route): Vodafone Ireland support page says there is a "dedicated Spam reporting line" for forwarding suspected spam texts free of charge; the number was cut off. A Digital Rights Ireland page (older, may be outdated) lists Vodafone 50005 alongside O2 and Meteor numbers. Three Ireland and Eir: no number found. https://n.vodafone.ie/support/code/security.html
- ACTION NEEDED: before any Irish forwarding number goes live, confirm it on the Vodafone, Eir, Three and Virgin/O2 support pages, or by phoning ComReg consumer line. Do not ship 50050 on current evidence.
- ComReg advice (search summary, unverified wording): do not click links or reply; delete the text; if you gave financial details, contact your bank and report to your local Garda station.

## United Kingdom

**Take Five to Stop Fraud** - https://www.takefive-stopfraud.org.uk/
- VERIFIED core advice: "Stop", "Challenge", "Protect".
  - Stop: "Take a moment to stop and think before parting with your money or information. It could keep you safe."
  - Challenge: "Ask yourself, could it be fake?" and "It's ok to reject, refuse or ignore any requests."
  - Protect: "Contact your bank immediately if you think you've been scammed" and report to police at reportfraud.police.uk or 0300 123 2040.
- VERIFIED recurring line: "We will never text or call you and ask you to share your information or transfer money."
- Note: the Take Five wording "Stop. Challenge. Protect." is from this page, not Irish sources.

**Stop Scams UK - 159** - http://stopscamsuk.org.uk/ (https redirects to http; the https fetch gave a redirect).
- VERIFIED only that a "159 Phone number" page exists at /our-programmes/159-phone-number/. The body was not retrieved.
- UNVERIFIED: 159 description. Do not describe the 159 service in the product until the programme page is read. Note: 159 is the UK number for connecting to a bank's fraud line (verify on the programme page before using).
- UNVERIFIED: 7726 reporting (see FTC/Ofcom below).

**7726 (SPAM)** - verified via the FTC page (see US section): forward phishing texts to SPAM (7726). This is the UK short code; it is cited by the FTC page for US consumers, so verify on Ofcom or the UK operator pages before using it for UK users.
